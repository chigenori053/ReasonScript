# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

ReasonScript contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate with
respect, welcome constructive technical disagreement, and avoid harassment,
discrimination, threats, and disclosure of another person’s private
information. Feedback should address ideas and observable behavior rather than
the person presenting them.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, and other spaces where a participant represents the ReasonScript
project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns
privately through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/chigenori053/ReasonScript/security/advisories/new).
Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled according to their context
and severity.
